\section{A little more on the descent condition}
\label{peupluspage}

We would like to have a slightly more explicit version of the limits that
enter into characterisation \ref{critereaveclim}.

Here we suppose that the site $\Xx$ admits fibre products.

We shall say that the site $\Xx$ admits {\em sufficiently many disjoint sums
compatible with fibre products} if there exists an ordinal $\beta$ such that
the sieves corresponding to the covering families of size strictly smaller
than $\beta$ generate the topology (i.e. are cofinal among all the sieves), and
if the disjoint sums of fewer than $\beta$ elements exist in $\Xx$ and are
compatible with fibre products (i.e. one has the distributivity formula for the
fibre product of two disjoint sums over an object of $X$).

For example, if $\Xx$ is quasi-compact, one may take $\beta = \omega$, and the
condition is that finite disjoint sums exist. For the site of paracompact
spaces one would take $\beta = \omega +1$.

Furthermore, we shall say that {\em disjoint sums are covering} if for every
family $\Uu$ whose disjoint sum $X$ exists, the family $\Uu$ is a family
covering $X$.

\begin{remark}
\label{casuffit}
In the sites considered here, for condition (b) of \ref{critere} or
\ref{critereaveclim} it suffices to take into account the sieves generated by
the covering families with fewer than $\beta$ elements.
\end{remark}
Indeed, these sieves generate the topology.

Suppose now that $\Uu = \{ U_{\alpha}\rightarrow X\}$ is a family covering
$X\in \Xx$, having fewer than $\beta$ elements. We set
$$
\amalg \Uu :=
\amalg _{\alpha} U_{\alpha}.
$$
It is an object of $\Xx /X$. However, the sieve generated by this object is
larger than the sieve generated by $\Uu$, and in fact the sieves generated by
the $\amalg \Uu$ do not in general form a cofinal family.

We therefore need the following definition.

\begin{definition}
\label{compsomdis}
Let $A$ be a Segal $n$-prestack on $\Xx$ whose values $A(U)$ are fibrant.
We shall say that {\em $A$ is compatible with disjoint sums} if for every family
$\Uu = \{ U_{\alpha} \}$ such that $\amalg \Uu$ exists, the natural morphism
$$
A(\amalg \Uu )\rightarrow \prod _{\alpha} A(U_{\alpha})
$$
is a weak equivalence.
\end{definition}

In our setting, where the ordinal $\beta$ is fixed, we shall simply say
``compatible with disjoint sums'', without specifying that this concerns only
the disjoint sums of fewer than $\beta$ elements.

Note in particular that the disjoint sum of the empty family (i.e. the family
indexed by $\emptyset$) is an initial object $\iota$ of the site $\Xx$
(generally the empty object $\iota = \emptyset$ if $\Xx$ is a site of geometric
objects), and the condition that $A$ be compatible with disjoint sums implies
$A(\iota )= \ast$.

One can understand condition \ref{compsomdis} with the help of the following
topology: we call {\em topology of connected components} the topology generated
by the families $\Uu \rightarrow X$ satisfying $X=\amalg \Uu$ (this condition
makes sense without one supposing the existence of all disjoint sums: it means
that $X$ is a disjoint sum of the elements of $\Uu$). The existence of fibre
products and the compatibility of these with disjoint sums guarantees that one
does indeed define a topology in this way.

\begin{remark}
\label{topcompcon}
Let $A$ be a Segal $n$-prestack on $\Xx$ whose values $A(U)$ are fibrant.
Then $A$ is compatible with disjoint sums if and only if $A$ is a stack for the
topology of connected components.
\end{remark}

Indeed, condition \ref{compsomdis} is the same as condition (c) of
\ref{critereaveclim}: the limit of $A$ over the sieve generated by $\Uu$ is
equivalent to $\prod _{\alpha} A(U_{\alpha})$ and, by Remark \ref{casuffit}, it
suffices to consider the sieves generated by covering families.

We shall now show that if $A$ is compatible with disjoint sums, then in
condition (b) of \ref{critere} or \ref{critereaveclim} it suffices to consider
the sieves generated by a covering family with $1$ element. More precisely, we
prove:

\begin{lemma}
\label{unseul}
Suppose that $\Xx$ admits sufficiently many disjoint sums.
Let $A$ be a Segal $n$-prestack on $\Xx$ fibrant object-by-object.
Suppose that $A$ is compatible with disjoint sums (\ref{compsomdis}) and
satisfies condition (a) of \ref{critereaveclim}, as well as condition (b) for
the sieves generated by the covering families with $1$ element. Then $A$ is a
Segal $n$-stack.
\end{lemma}
{\em Proof:}
One may suppose $A$ fibrant for the coarse topology. By Remark
\ref{topcompcon}, $A$ is also fibrant for the topology of connected components.
Let $\Bb$ be a sieve generated by a covering family $\Uu \rightarrow X$. Let
$\Bb '$ be the sieve generated by $\amalg \Uu$; one has $\Bb \subset \Bb '$.
The morphism
$$
\ast _{\Bb}\rightarrow \ast _{\Bb '}
$$
is a trivial cofibration for the topology of connected components.
Thus if $f:\ast _{\Bb}\rightarrow A$ is a morphism, there exists an extension of
it to $f':\ast _{\Bb '}\rightarrow A$. One has at one's disposal condition (b)
of \ref{critereaveclim} for $\Bb '$, which implies that of \ref{critere}, and it
follows that this morphism $f'$ extends to a morphism
$\ast _{\Xx /X}\rightarrow A$. One thus obtains condition (b) for every sieve
$\Bb$ generated by a covering family, which suffices (by Remark
\ref{casuffit}).
\eop

\bigskip

We still suppose that $\Xx$ admits direct products and sufficiently many
disjoint sums. From now on we fix $X\in \Xx$ and a covering family $\Uu$ which
consists of a single element $U\rightarrow X$. We denote by $\Bb$ the sieve
generated by $\Uu$. We define a functor
$$
\rho (\Uu ): \Delta ^o \rightarrow \Xx
$$
by
$$
\rho (\Uu )(p):= U\times \ldots \times U \;\;\;\; p+1\;\;
\mbox{times}.
$$

We shall rewrite condition (b) of \ref{critereaveclim} once more. We suppose
that $A$ is a Segal $n$-prestack, and we have $\rho (\Uu
)^{\ast }A$, which is a Segal $n$-prestack on $\Delta ^0$, i.e. a cosimplicial
Segal $n$-precategory, or again a functor from $\Delta$ to the Segal
$n$-precategories. On the other hand, let
$$
t: \rho (\Uu )\rightarrow \underline{X}
$$
be the natural transformation from the functor $\Delta ^o\rightarrow \Xx$ to the
constant functor with value $X$, which comes from the projections $\Uu \times
_X\ldots \times _X\Uu \rightarrow X$. If one denotes by $c^{\ast} A(X)$ the
constant cosimplicial Segal $n$-precategory (i.e. the constant functor from
$\Delta$ to the Segal $n$-precategories with value $A(X)$), then $t$ induces a
morphism
$$
t^{\ast} : c^{\ast}A(X) \rightarrow  \rho (\Uu )^{\ast }A
$$
of cosimplicial Segal $n$-precategories.

In particular, $t$ induces a morphism
$$
t^{\ast} : A(X) \rightarrow \lim _{\leftarrow , \Delta} \rho (\Uu )^{\ast }A.
$$
Here the limit is the one of \cite{limits}. By the construction of \cite{limits}
recalled in \S 14 above, one may write
$$
\lim _{\leftarrow , \Delta} \rho (\Uu )^{\ast }A
= \underline{Hom}(\Delta , nSeCAT')_{1/}(\ast , \rho (\Uu )^{\ast }A).
$$
Here $nSeCAT'$ denotes a fibrant replacement of $nSeCAT$.

\begin{corollary}
Let $\rho (\Uu )^{\ast }A\rightarrow B$ be an object-by-object weak equivalence
to a Segal $n$-prestack over $\Delta ^o$ fibrant for the coarse topology. Then
\newline
$\lim _{\leftarrow , \Delta} \rho (\Uu )^{\ast }A$
is equivalent to the Segal $n$-precategory $\Gamma (\Delta ^o, B)$ of global
sections of the presheaf $B$.
\end{corollary}
{\em Proof:}
This is a direct consequence of Proposition \ref{calclim}.
\eop

We learned the following lemma in the book of Dwyer--Hirschhorn--Kan
\cite{DHK}, but it is due to Bousfield--Kan (\cite{BousfieldKan} Ch.~XI \S 9).
We give a version slightly weaker than what one finds in
\cite{BousfieldKan} \cite{DHK}. We shall say that a functor
$f:\Yy \rightarrow \Zz$ is {\em strongly initial} if for every object
$Z\in \Zz$ the category $f/Z$ is filtering (for comparison, in
{\em loc.\ cit.} $f$ is said to be {\em initial} if the $f/Z$ have contractible
nerve).

\begin{lemma}
If $f:\Yy \rightarrow \Zz$ is a functor of $1$-categories which is strongly
initial, then for every functor $G: \Zz \rightarrow nSeCAT'$ the natural morphism
$$ \lim _{\leftarrow , \Zz } G \stackrel{\cong}{\rightarrow}
\lim _{\leftarrow , \Yy } f^{\ast}G
$$
is an equivalence of Segal $n$-categories.
\end{lemma}
{\em Proof:}
By \ref{strictif3}
\footnote{There is no circularity here, inasmuch as if one uses this lemma
before the proof of \ref{strictif3}, it will be on a $G$ which is already
strict.}
one may suppose that $G$ comes from a Segal $n$-prestack fibrant for the coarse
topology of $\Zz$.

The pullback $f^{\ast}G$ is then fibrant for the coarse topology of $\Yy$.
Indeed, it suffices to show that $f_!$ preserves trivial cofibrations. One has
the formula
$$
f_!(U)(Z)= \lim _{\rightarrow , f/Z} U|_{f/Z},
$$
and since by hypothesis $f/Z$ is filtering, this colimit preserves cofibrations
and trivial cofibrations.

One proves the lemma first for $n=0$, i.e. for Segal $0$-categories or
simplicial sets. In this case, by Proposition \ref{calclim}, the limits in
question are equal to $\Gamma (\Zz , G)$ and $\Gamma (\Yy , f^{\ast}G)$
respectively. Now these are equivalent to the $holim_{\Zz}(G)$ and
$holim_{\Yy}(f^{\ast}G)$ of Bousfield--Kan \cite{BousfieldKan}. The property in
question for these $holim$ (\cite{BousfieldKan} Ch.~XI theorem 9.2) gives the
statement.

For arbitrary $n$ one can say exactly the same thing, using the extension by
Dwyer--Hirschhorn--Kan of the $holim$ of Bousfield--Kan to a more general model
category, in this case the category of Segal $n$-precategories.

Another way of completing the argument for arbitrary $n$ is to note that one
has
$$
\Gamma (\Zz , G)^{int, 0}=
\Gamma (\Zz , G^{int, 0})
$$
and likewise
$$
\Gamma (\Yy , f^{\ast}G)^{int, 0}=
\Gamma (\Yy , (f^{\ast}G)^{int, 0});
$$
it follows that
$$
\Gamma (\Zz , G)^{int, 0}\rightarrow
\Gamma (\Yy , f^{\ast}G)^{int, 0}
$$
is an equivalence. In particular the functor in question is essentially
surjective.

On the other hand, for $x,y\in \Gamma (\Zz , G)$ one has
$$
\Gamma (\Zz , G)_{1/}(x,y) =
\Gamma (\Zz , G_{1/}(x,y))
$$
and $G_{1/}(x,y)$ is an $n-1$-prestack fibrant over $\Zz$ (since the lifting
property for $G_{1/}(x,y)$ with respect to a trivial cofibration
$E\rightarrow E'$ is implied by the same property for $G$ with respect to the
trivial cofibration $\Upsilon (E)\rightarrow \Upsilon (E')$); and likewise for
$(f^{\ast}G )_{1/}(f^{\ast}x, f^{\ast}y)$. By induction, one may suppose the
lemma known for the $n-1$-precategories of Segal, whence one obtains that
$$
\Gamma (\Zz , G_{1/}(x,y))\rightarrow \Gamma (\Yy , (f^{\ast}G)_{1/}(f^{\ast}x,
f^{\ast}y))
$$
is an equivalence, which implies that
$$
\Gamma (\Zz , G)_{1/}(x,y)\rightarrow
\Gamma (\Yy , f^{\ast}G)_{1/}(f^{\ast}x, f^{\ast}y)
$$
is an equivalence and the functor in question is fully faithful.
\eop

{\em Remark--Question:}
Bousfield--Kan and Dwyer--Hirschhorn--Kan give the same statement for colimits
in a closed model category $M$, with only the hypothesis that $f$ is initial,
i.e. that the $f/Z$ have contractible nerve. Can one obtain the same statement
here? In the proof above, we used the condition ``strongly initial'' in order to
say that $f_!$ preserves cofibrations and trivial cofibrations.

\begin{corollary}
\label{limsurdelta}
Suppose that $\Xx$ admits fibre products.
Let $A$ be a Segal $n$-prestack, and let $\Bb \subset
\Xx /X$
be the sieve associated to a covering family $\Uu \rightarrow X$.
Then one has a natural equivalence
$$
\lim _{\leftarrow , \Bb ^o} A|_{\Bb} \stackrel{\cong}{\rightarrow}
\lim _{\leftarrow , \Delta  } \rho (\Uu )^{\ast}A.
$$
\end{corollary}
{\em Proof:}
By the lemma, it suffices to verify that the morphism
$$
\rho (\Uu ): \Delta \rightarrow \Bb ^o
$$
is initial. For $Y\in \Bb ^o$, the category $\rho (\Uu )/Y$
is equal to the category
$$
\{ (n,f): \;\;\; n\in \Delta , \;\;\; f: Y\rightarrow P_n(\Uu )\} .
$$
A morphism $f: Y\rightarrow P_n(\Uu )$ is the same thing as an
$n+1$-tuple of morphisms $Y\rightarrow \Uu$ over $X$. Let $H:= Hom
(Y,\Uu )$ be the set of morphisms $Y\rightarrow \Uu$ over $X$,
and let us adopt the notation $P_n(H)$ for the product
of $n+1$ copies of $H$ (which defines a
simplicial set $P_{\cdot}(H)$ that is the nerve of the
category having $H$
as its set of objects and an isomorphism between each pair of objects). The
category $\rho (\Uu )/Y$ is therefore equal to
$$
\{ (n,h): \;\;\; n\in \Delta , \;\;\; h\in P_n(H)\} ,
$$
in other words it is the total category associated to
the simplicial set
$P_{\cdot}(H)$,
which is contractible because $H$ is non-empty (the condition $H\neq \emptyset$
is equivalent to the condition $Y\in \Bb$, this being the definition of the
sieve $\Bb$ associated to $\Uu$). By the lemma, one obtains the equivalence
between limits sought.
\eop

\begin{proposition}
\label{critere7}
Suppose that $\Xx$ admits fibre products, and sufficiently many
\linebreak
disjoint sums compatible with fibre products. Let $A$ be a
Segal $n$-prestack
such that $A(X)$ is a Segal $n$-category for every $X\in \Xx$.
Then $A$ is a stack if and only if:
\newline
(a) \,  for every $X\in \Xx$ and every pair of objects $x,y\in A_0(X)$, the
Segal $n-1$-prestack $A_{1/}(x,y)$ is a Segal $n-1$-stack over
$\Xx /X$;
and
\newline
(b)\, $A$ is compatible with disjoint sums (Definition
\ref{compsomdis}), and for every $X$ and every covering family $\Uu \rightarrow
X$ with one element, the morphism
$$
t^{\ast} : A(X) \rightarrow \lim _{\leftarrow , \Delta} \rho (\Uu )^{\ast }A
$$
defined above is essentially surjective.

Condition (b) may be
replaced by the same condition for any one of the
$A^{int, k}$.

%%%% the following condition is restricted in v3 to the n-truncated
%%%%%% case &&&&&&&&&&&&&
If $A$ is an $n$-prestack (not a Segal one) then
the fact that $A$ is a stack is also equivalent to the condition
\newline
(c)\,\,  $A$ is compatible with disjoint sums (Definition
\ref{compsomdis}), and for every $X$ and every covering family $\Uu
\rightarrow
X$ with one element, the
morphism
$$
t^{\ast} : A(X) \rightarrow \lim _{\leftarrow , \Delta} \rho (\Uu )^{\ast }A
$$
is an equivalence of Segal $n$-categories.
\end{proposition}
{\em Proof:}
Combine Corollaries \ref{critereaveclim} and \ref{limsurdelta}.
\eop

If one replaces condition (b) by the same condition for the simplicial
presheaf $A^{int, 0}$, then the limit
in question is the same as the $holim$ of Bousfield--Kan. One
can therefore rewrite:
\begin{corollary}
Suppose that $\Xx$ admits fibre products, and sufficiently many disjoint sums
compatible with fibre products. Let $A$ be a Segal $n$-prestack such that
$A(X)$ is a Segal $n$-category
for every $X\in \Xx$. Then $A$ is a stack if and only if:
\newline
(a) \,  for every $X\in \Xx$ and every pair of objects $x,y\in A_0(X)$, the
Segal $n-1$-prestack $A_{1/}(x,y)$ is a Segal $n-1$-stack over
$\Xx /X$; and
\newline
(b)\, $A$ is compatible with disjoint sums (Definition
\ref{compsomdis}), and for every $X$ and every covering family $\Uu \rightarrow
X$ with one element, the morphism
$$
t^{\ast} : A^{int, 0}(X) \rightarrow holim _{\leftarrow , \Delta} \rho
(\Uu )^{\ast }A^{int ,0}
$$
induces a surjection on the $\pi _0$.

If $A$ is a stack then the morphism of (b) is a weak equivalence.
\end{corollary}
\eop

This criterion allows one---by induction on $n$---to grasp the condition for
being a stack in terms of simplicial presheaves only, and in this framework the
notion is already well known.
