\section{Preschemes and Abelian Categories}

--- Let $(X, \mathcal{O}_X)$ be a noetherian prescheme. We shall see
that the data, up to equivalence, of the category $\mathbf{F}_X$ allows
one to reconstruct the prescheme $(X, \mathcal{O}_X)$; for this, we
shall say that a localizing subcategory $\mathbf{A}$ of
$\mathbf{F}_X$ is \emph{finite} if there exists a noetherian object $M$
such that $\mathbf{A}$ is the smallest localizing subcategory
containing $M$; it amounts to the same to say that $\mathbf{A}$ is of
the form $\operatorname{Cat} R$, where $R$ is a closed subset of $X$.

Theorem~1 establishes a bijective correspondence between $X$ and the
spectrum $\operatorname{Sp}(\mathbf{F}_X)$ of $\mathbf{F}_X$. In this
correspondence, the open subsets correspond to the spectra
$\operatorname{Sp}(\mathbf{F}_X/\mathbf{A})$, where $\mathbf{A}$ ranges
over the \emph{finite} localizing subcategories of $\mathbf{F}_X$.
Precisely, the open subset $U$ corresponds to
$\operatorname{Sp}(\mathbf{F}_X/\mathbf{A}(U))$ if $\mathbf{A}(U)$
denotes the subcategory consisting of the $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules whose
support does not meet $U$.

The sets $\operatorname{Sp}(\mathbf{F}_X/\mathbf{A})$ defining on
$\operatorname{Sp}(\mathbf{F}_X)$ a topological space structure, it
remains to equip this topological space with a sheaf of rings $O$: by
the end of Section~2, one may take $Z[\mathbf{F}_X/\mathbf{A}]$ as the
ring of sections of $O$ over
$\operatorname{Sp}(\mathbf{F}_X/\mathbf{A})$. If $\mathbf{A}$ is
contained in $\mathbf{B}$, the canonical functor from
$\mathbf{F}_X/\mathbf{A}$ to $\mathbf{F}_X/\mathbf{B}$ induces a
homomorphism from $Z[\mathbf{F}_X/\mathbf{A}]$ to
$Z[\mathbf{F}_X/\mathbf{B}]$ (cf.\ \S2); it is this homomorphism which
is chosen for restriction homomorphism.
